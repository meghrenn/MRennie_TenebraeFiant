Luck

In the foyer of the LINC building there’s a statue of a pronghorn.

Some people say its name is Kevin.

Legend has it that you can pet it for good luck: the snout for exams and the rear for sports matches. I recommend you try either—neither is quite a match for our situation, but if this plot goes as deep as I think it does, we'll need all the luck we can get.
